# Station 4: The Eye of Shadow

> Sometimes the eye wants the room. Sometimes it wants a secret.

Let your badge wake the challenge. First, give the eye a clear look at the light around you.

Then follow the blue guide: when it keeps watch, the eye prefers a hiding place; when it disappears, let the room be seen. Green means you are on the right path.

**Hint:** Hold each answer steady. Flickering truth is no truth at all.
